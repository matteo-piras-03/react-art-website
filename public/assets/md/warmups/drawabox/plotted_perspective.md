1) Divide the page in 3 rectangles using a ruler.
2) From here on out, on each frame, draw an horizon line and two vanishing points on either end, close to the edge of the frame.
3) Draw a vertical edge, perpendicular to the horizon line.
4) Plot its corners back to the vanishing points.
5) Place two outer vertical edges.
6) Plot them back to the vanishing points.
7) Connect the final two corners for the back vertical edge (not necessarily with a vertical line).
8) Repeat this process 5-6 times per frame.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to introduce students to the idea of working with vanishing points, and also getting used to have a lot of lines on the page.
