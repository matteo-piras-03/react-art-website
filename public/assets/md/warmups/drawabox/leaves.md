1) Establish a center flow line, focusing on movement and energy, drawing with **confidence**.
2) Enclose the leaf with simple curves, drawn with **confidence**.
3) Using reference images, apply complex edge details to the leaves, adding individual bumps one at a time.
4) Optionally and finally, add details using cast shadows techniques taught in Lesson 2's [Texture Analysis](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/textureanalysis) exercise.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Stiff lines
> Make sure the center flow line and the enclosure lines are drawn with confidence.

### Not folding naturally
> Make sure to twist and turn some of the leaves in three dimensions.

### Skipping constructional steps
> Leaves with many different arm should go through multiple constructional steps, by giving each arm its own flow line and enclosures.

### Zig-zagging edge detail
> Do not apply a single continuous zig-zagging stroke to represent edge detail.
