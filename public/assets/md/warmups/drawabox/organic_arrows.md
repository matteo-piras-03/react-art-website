1) Draw an S curve. This curve will establish the negative space occupied.
2) Draw the second edge. This edge will establish the positive space.
3) Close off the structure, adding an arrow head on one end.
4) Finally, add hatching at the bends.

>**Optionally for warmups**: Add line weight on line overlaps.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Being afraid to let edges overlap
> This is going to make the arrows look very flat.

### Not applying perspective
> Apply foreshortening to the positive and negative space.

## Goal

This exercise is meant to familiarize students with the idea of having a three-dimensional space, rather than a flat 2d canvas.
