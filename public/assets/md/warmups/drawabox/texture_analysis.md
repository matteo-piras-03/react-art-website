4) The final column is reserved for a transition gradient from dark to light. Place a solid black column on the left first, then redraw the texture applying a transition gradient.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Scribbling
> Make sure to take your time when analyzing the texture and not rush.

### Drawing the forms of a texture
> Make sure to not outline the forms present on the texture, rather, rely on the cast shadows instead.

### No transition from dense to sparse
> The dark and white bars should not be explicitely visible, as there should be a gradual transition between solid black and solid white.


## Goal

The purpose of this exercise is how to study a reference and how to organize information from said reference.
