Official website page [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/ghostedplanes).

1) Place four dots on the page.
2) **Using the ghosting method**, connect the four dots to form a plane. 
3) Also construct lines between the corners of the plane (or the diagonals).
4) Bisect the plane in both dimensions by drawing a cross through the center of the X created in the previous step.

>**Optional for warmups**: Envision these planes as being three dimensional rectilinear surfaces, or in other words a rectangle or square floating in 3d space.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is the same as the ghosted lines exercise, but for more complex shapes, to get used to slowing down and thinking about units of work, which are lines.
