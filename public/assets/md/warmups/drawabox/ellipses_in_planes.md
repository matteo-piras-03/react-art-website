1) Get yourself a page of the [Ghosted Planes](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/ghostedplanes) exercise.
2) Draw an ellipse within each plane.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Deformed ellipse
> Draw ellipses with confidence trying to maintain a smooth elliptical shape first.

### Floating ellipse
> Avoid drawing "floating" ellipses, that is, ellipses that do not touch any side of the plane.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to practice drawing ellipses within a plane, an useful skill for constructing cylinders inside of boxes.
