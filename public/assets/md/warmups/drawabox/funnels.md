1) Draw a long line (representing the minor axis) with a shorter one marking out its middle, using a ruler.
2) Along either side of the long line, draw an arc, with round objects found around the house.
3) Finally, in the space inside of the funnel, draw ellipses, **keeping the minor axis aligned to the guide**.

>**Optional for warmups**: Try to increase the degree of the ellipses as you move outwards from the center, keeping a narrow degree towards the center and a wide degree towords the outside.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Minor axis not aligned
> Draw ellipses aligned to the central minor axis line.

### Poor spacing
> Try to keep ellipses snugly against one another, without leaving any space between them.

### Being too loose
> Try to keep ellipses snugly within the funnel shape.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to get used to the use of the minor axis line, an useful skill for some Lesson 2 exercises.
