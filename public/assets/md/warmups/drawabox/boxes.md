1) Using the [Y Method](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/organicperspective/ymethod), draw a series of boxes with either rapid or shallow convergence.
2) Repeat this process 4-5 times per page.
3) Check the convergences of the edges by extending them.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Lines parallel on the page
> Make sure all of the boxes' lines converge. The Y method ensures that the boxes' lines cannot be parallel on the page.

### Line extensions pointing towards the viewer
> It is extremely important that the line extensions point away from the viewer, as their purpose is to help us analyze how those lines converge or not to a vanishing point.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to get used to drawing freely rotated boxes without explicit vanishing points.
