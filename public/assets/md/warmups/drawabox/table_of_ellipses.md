1) Divide the page into a table with two columns and a bunch of rows.
2) Using the ghosting method, draw ellipses of **various degrees** within each cell. Draw through the ellipses **two full times** before lifting the pen.
3) Optionally, you can also draw a wave through the section, and filling the space between the wave and the table.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Drawing without a concrete goal
> Avoid drawing "floating" ellipses, that is, ellipses that do not touch any element of the table and/or other ellipses.

### Not drawing through the ellipses
> Ellipses drawn in a single round usually come out uneven and wobbly.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to practice drawing ellipses in a variety of sizes, orientations and degrees. It also sets out a clear space each ellipse is meant to occupy, giving us a means to assess wether or not an ellipse was successful or not.
