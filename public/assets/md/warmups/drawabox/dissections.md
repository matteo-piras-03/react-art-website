Official website page [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/dissections).

1) Get yourself a page of the [Sausages with Contour Ellipses](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/contourlines) exercise.
2) Pick a section of the organic form.
3) Gather texture references, and apply the textures to the chosen sections, focusing on **wrapping the texture** around the section and also **breaking the silhouette**.
4) Repeat this until the page is full.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Not minding the curvature
> The texture needs to get compressed and warped along the sides as the surface turns away from the viewer.

### Not breaking the silhouette
> Punching through the silhouette is going to have the biggest impact.
