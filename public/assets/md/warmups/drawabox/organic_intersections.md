Official website page [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/organicintersections).

1) Draw a simple and big organic form.
2) Reinforce the volume of said form with **contour lines**.
3) Add another **simple** organic form imagining it dangling on top of the first form.
4) Repeat this process a couple of times.
5) Finally, add line weight and cast shadows.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Using complicated forms
> Make sure to not use them as it increases the complexity of the exercise.

### Laying sausages in parallel
> Lay out sausages cross-wise, in opposite orientations. This makes the form stabilized and gives an opportunity to wrap around the form beneath it.

### Shadow not wrapping to the surface it falls upon
> Do not have the shadow adhere to the form casting it, make sure it wraps around the surface of the organic form.

## Goal

The purpose of this exercise is to develop the understanding of 3d space and how forms exists within it in relation to each other, in a different manner from the Forms Intersections exercise.
