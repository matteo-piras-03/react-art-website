1) **Planning phase**: place two dots on the page, representing the ends of the mark that needs to be placed.
2) **Preparation phase**: rotate the page where to ensure the orientation is the most comfortable one. Start ghosting through the mark that needs to be placed.
3) **Execution phase**: Execute the mark with confidence, without hesitation and second guessing.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Wobbling
> A wobbly line suggests hesitation and conscious brain usage, which has to be avoided.

### Arcing lines
> An arcing line suggests that you're not drawing from the shoulder. If it happens while doing so, consider applying an arcing motion in the opposite direction.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to slow down and develop a habit of thinking before drawing, and to consider what purpose the mark serves and wether or not it is the best mark for that purpose.
