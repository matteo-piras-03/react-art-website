1) Draw a series of straight lines first with the ruler, varying in size. You can also try arcing lines and some waves, though this will increase the difficulty.
2) Repeat the same stroke freehand 8 times.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Wobbling
> A wobbly line suggests hesitation and conscious brain usage, which has to be avoided.

### Fraying on both ends
> Fraying on both ends suggests that time is not taken to position then pen correctly at the starting point of the guideline.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to draw lines with confidence, since the hand blocks the view of the guidelines.
