Official website page [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/roughperspective).

1) Divide the page in 3 rectangles using a ruler.
2) From here on out, on each frame, draw an horizon line and one vanishing point placed to the center.
3) Draw a rectangle, place the back corners of the box, in such a way that these new edges **follow the vanishing point**. You can ghost back and forth from the box's corner to the vanishing point.
4) Finally, construct all of the edges of the box.
5) Repeat this process 4-5 times per frame.

Once the page is filled, check if your convergences are correct by **extending either the edges or the corners**.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Plotting lines back to the vanishing point
> Don't plot actual lines back to the vanishing points, do that with the ghosting between the box's corner and the vanishing point.

### Extending lines incorrectly
> Do not extend the front corner to the vanishing point, as this doesn't tell how far off you were from the vanishing point.

## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to separate students from the explicit reliance of plotting back to the vanishing points, and getting used to the fact that parallel lines converge.
