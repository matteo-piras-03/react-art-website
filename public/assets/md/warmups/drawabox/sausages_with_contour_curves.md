1) Draw a sausage form, consisting of two spheres connected by a consistent central tube (don't draw these forms explicitely).
2) Draw a minor axis line through the sausage, trying your best to keep it centered.
3) Then, you can add a few contour ellipses, **varying in degrees**, or you can add contour lines instead, making sure to overshoot the curves towards the edges.
4) Finally, add an ellipse on the tip of the sausage.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Inconsistent sausage shapes
> Make sure that the ends are spherical and have similar size, and the midsections of the sausages don't get narrower or wider.

### Curves/ellipses floating
> It is extremely important that the ellipses / contour lines fit snugly between the edges of the organic form, otherwise it breaks the 3D illusion.

### Same degrees all over the sausage
> Give the ellipses a degree shift towards the length of the form to reinforce the 3D illusion.

### Ellipses misaligned
> Each ellipse should be aligned to the minor axis line drawn on the page, similar to the requirements of the [Funnels](https://drawabox.com/lesson/1/funnels) exercise.

### Contour curves too shallow
> Towards the end, the contour curve needs to accelerate the curvature, to reinforce the 3D illusion.

## Goal

This exercise shows us that contour lines reinforce the illusion of three-dimensionality of a form and gives us a better understanding on how these forms exist in 3d space.
