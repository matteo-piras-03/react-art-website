Official website page [here](https://drawabox.com/lesson/2/formintersections).

1) Draw a series of solid forms (boxes, cylinders, spheres, cones and pyramids) **roughly equilateral** and with **shallow convergence**, making sure they **overlap** on one another, forming a big group.
2) Inside the overlap between said solid forms, define the form intersections.

## Mistakes to avoid

### Using stretched forms
> Make sure to not use them as it increases the complexity of the exercise.

### Small groups of forms
> Make sure to draw one big chunk of forms that are all overlapping.

### Using a cleanup pass
> Do not draw forms lightly / faintly before going back over the lines with a rich dark line.

## Goal

The purpose of this exercise is to develop the understanding of 3d space and how forms exists within it in relation to each other. The primary goal  here is to draw a bunch of forms that all look and feel consistent. or that they feel like they belong together in the same space and scene.
