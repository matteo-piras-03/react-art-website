# Why did I start to draw

Browsing on Instagram I would occasionally stumble on realistic paintings with somewhat realistic environments, but there were key elements that made the picture unreal.
And I have to say that these kinds of drawings are what inspired me on the first place, but really didn't get me drawing. I have not really drew previously
before November 2025, but safe to say I was the best student when it came to technical drawing (mostly axonometry of solid forms) in high school.

Fast forward to university, during the master's degree, I sat beside a colleague who drew creatures during boring lectures as a way to pass the time. Amazed by this
skill, I figured it would be a great idea to grab myself an Ipad and started taking notes digitally. I also bought procreate, and tried to do a portrait of my cat,
which in my opinion it turned out really cool!

After realizing I somewhat got potential on this (and also, living among an artistic family, given that my grandma is a painter, my granddad is a blacksmith, my cousin and my mom draw)
I started to search on ways to seriously improve. I stumbled upon [Marc Brunet's 30 day drawing challenge](https://youtu.be/6gAnatlxcPs), and decided to give it a shot, mostly
to develop a consistent drawing habit, which it did work (and boosted my drawing skills).

After that, I found [Drawabox](https://drawabox.com) and decided to stick with Uncomfortable's questionable advice. While questionable, it grew my desire
to draw so much to the point I would usually draw for 1-2 hours every day. At the time of writing this, I have almost completed lesson 5, and I've planned to complete
the course.

# What do I like to draw

Some of the areas I like are Character design, Creature design and Environment design. I don't really have big goals for these areas, just doing something
cool and pretty is good enough for me. These days I mostly draw anthropomorphic characters set in a somewhat detailed background.
